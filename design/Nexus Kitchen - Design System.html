02Two signals: state & verdict

The system keeps two signals deliberately apart. State is where a meal sits in the supply chain right now — have it, can make it, or need to get; it's derived from inventory and drives every operation. Verdict is how the meal was — a retrospective signal that travels with the dish so you remember winners and don't re-cook duds.

Signal 1 · State — “Where is this meal in the supply chain?”
Have it
A ready-to-eat portion already exists. Zero steps — just eat.
Can make it
A recipe whose ingredients are all on hand. One step — cook it.
Need to get
Missing ingredients, or a store-bought to buy. Shop, then it moves up.
Have it Can make it Need to get

The whole product exists to move every requirement to “Have it” before its day. State is derived, never stored — and shown with an icon + label, never color alone.

Signal 2 · Verdict — “How was the meal?”
Keeper
“Again, please”
You'd happily repeat it. Surfaces first in suggestions; repeat keepers fill your Keepers list.
It was fine
Neutral — stays quiet
No mark travels with the meal. Fine to plan again; nothing is flagged either way.
Set aside
“Not for me”
Quietly drops out of suggestions — never deleted. Resting, never rejected; reuses energy-1's periwinkle.
The capture — calm, optional, retrospective:

Asked once after a meal is logged — never required, editable anytime. Like state: icon + word, never color alone; no thumbs-down red.

06Voice & tone

Shame-free is not configurable — it's embedded everywhere. Neutral framing, gaps without judgment, and an easy way out of every flow.

Never

"You missed lunch again."
"You broke your 6-day streak!"
"You're over your calorie goal."
"You haven't logged in 3 days."

Instead

"Here when you're ready to eat."
"6 days logged — nice. Breaks are fine."
"Here's the nutrition, just as info."
"Welcome back. Want to pick up where you left off?"
